# Lorax Hotline — Demo Plan

### 1. User texts the Lorax

**User:**
“Should I get a juicy beef burger for lunch?”

**Lorax:**
“Absolutely not, tree-hugger. 🪵 Beef production is a major driver of Amazon deforestation. Get a soy burger instead—it’s way yummier. Probably. 🌱”

### 2. User asks why

**User:**
“Wait, why is beef bad for forests?”

**Lorax:**
“Because forests get cleared for cattle pasture and for growing feed. Companies like **JBS, Minerva Foods, and Marfrig** are major players in the beef supply chain. The trees are not thrilled.”

### 3. User asks about a company

**User:**
“Is Minerva Foods actually bad for forests?”

**Lorax:**
“I’ll investigate. 🌳🔎”

The agent:

* Searches for information about Minerva Foods.
* Finds relevant sources.
* Validates the source URLs.
* Saves the sourced claims to the company's local dossier.

### 4. Lorax gives the sourced result

**Lorax:**
“Minerva is exposed to deforestation risk through its South American cattle supply chain. Here are the sources I found: [sources].”

### 5. User requests an action kit

**User:**
“Pester Minerva.”

**Lorax:**
“Fine. I have prepared your forest-defense kit. 🌲”

The agent prepares:

* Key sourced claims
* Relevant sources
* A draft message to the company
* Suggested actions the user can take

### 6. Human remains in control

**Lorax:**
“Draft ready. I will **not** send anything to Minerva. You decide whether to use it.”

**Demo takeaway:**
The demo shows the complete intended flow: **text → research → source validation → company dossier → action-kit draft**, while keeping actual company outreach human-controlled.
